Task 1 - Housing Prices Near Transit
- Number of records: 414
- Expected slope: about -0.73 price units per additional 100 metres.
- Expected prediction at 500 metres: about 42.22 price units.
- Interpretation: the negative slope means predicted house price per unit area
  decreases as distance from the nearest MRT station increases.

Task 2 - Yacht Resistance
- Number of experiments: 308
- Expected linear mean CV RMSE: about 8.95.
- Expected quadratic mean CV RMSE: about 4.19.
- Better model: Quadratic Regression, because its mean RMSE is lower.

Task 3 - Concrete Strength
- After removing duplicates: 1005 rows.
- Expected training rows: 804.
- Expected test rows: 201.
- Expected linear baseline mean CV RMSE: about 10.20 MPa.
- Expected tuned model mean CV RMSE: about 6.09 MPa.
- Expected tuned test RMSE: about 6.52 MPa.
- Example degree-2 interaction: Cement * Age.
- Scaling belongs inside the pipeline so each cross-validation training fold
  learns its own scaling values and validation data does not leak into training.
